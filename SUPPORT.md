# Burn15 support

Website: https://burn15.com/

Contact oxygenDrop at danielbarassistant@gmail.com for help or privacy questions. Include the operation, approximate time, and a redacted error description. Never include secret note text or a complete Burn15 link in a public issue.

The plugin requires Node.js 24 or newer and outbound HTTPS access to burn15.com. Notes expire 15 minutes after creation. A timed-out create may have succeeded; the helper deliberately does not automatically retry creation.

This free experimental utility has no promised uptime or response-time service level. See [privacy and deletion details](PRIVACY.md) before sharing sensitive information.
